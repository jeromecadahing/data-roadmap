    Habits that make it stick

    These cost nothing and matter as much as any course.

    Use AI as a study partner

    Ask it to explain a query, find a bug or check your reasoning. Always make sure you understand the answer before you use it.

    Check your data access

    Ask your manager or the data team what you can query, especially anything with customer or health information, and whether there's a sandbox for practice.

    Make it visible

    Share what you build, ask the data team for feedback and tell your manager what you're working toward.

    Keep a steady rhythm

    Study six days a week and rest on the seventh. Spend most sessions on the course and a couple each week applying it at work or on a project. If you miss a day, pick up the next day rather than doubling up.
